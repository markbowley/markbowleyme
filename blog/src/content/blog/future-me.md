Hey you, it’s...you, six months from now.

I want to say thanks.

Yeah right now, progress feels slow. Some days it feels like you're not moving at all right?

Here’s what I know that you don’t: those small wins you’re logging on Winnny[dot]com? They matter more than you realise.

That feedback, that task you crushed. The morning you stuck to your routine. They're not just checkboxes. They’re proof you’re getting somewhere.

Right now I’m looking back at our past six months, and I can see the progress. All those tiny wins weren’t nothing. They built momentum. Confidence. A stronger version of...us.

Half of them we’d have forgotten if we hadn’t logged them.

We’re all terrible at remembering our own progress. That's why logging them matters.

On the tough days ahead, (and yeah there are a few) you’ll need evidence that you’re capable. You’ll need proof you’ve overcome challenges before.

So thank you for tracking the small stuff even when it felt pointless.

From where I’m sitting, those moments mattered.

Keep going. Keep logging.

Future-you (me) is counting on it.
