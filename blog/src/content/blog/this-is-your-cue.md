If you've been reading this newsletter for a while but never replied… today's the day.

Every week, people tell me they've been reading for ages. But they've never responded.

Which means there's a small, invisible network here. And invisible networks aren't helping anyone.

I'm curious who's actually on the other side of this. I suspect there's a quiet group of smart people here who've never said hello.

So let's fix that.

Get in touch and tell me: what you're building, what you're stuck on, or just say hello. I read everything.

Let's make this a little less one-way.
