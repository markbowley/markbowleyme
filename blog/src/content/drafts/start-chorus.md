It can be so frustrating when you're starting out, or a solo creator.

No one knows who you are. No one’s waiting for your next thing. 
You don’t have access to the audience.
You have to prove yourself instantly.

Nile Rodgers had this problem in the 70s.

Black artists got less radio airplay. 
Less time to prove themselves. 
Less chances to build an audience.

So he developed a strategy: “Don't bore us, get to the chorus”.

Start with the hook. Lead with the payoff. 
Make it a banger from the first note.

No slow builds. 
No hoping the bridge justifies the intro. 

Without an established crowd, you can’t afford to explain yourself slowly.
You have to show real value immediately.

Your product demo needs to land. 
Your website headline needs to be clear in 3 seconds. 
Your first impression is the only impression.

Clarity isn’t optional.
Speed isn’t optional.

Maybe we should all start with the chorus.
