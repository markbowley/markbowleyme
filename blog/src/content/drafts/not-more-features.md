Most people don’t wake up wanting more app features.

They want less chaos.

Less time clicking through tools. Less friction between thought and action. Less distraction and hijacking whilst getting something done.

They want things to just WORK.

## The wrong question

Many of us builders tend to ask: ”How can I add more?”.

But the better question is “What can I remove?”. 

Or even “What friction can I remove?”.

Because the opportunity isn’t in giving people MORE to manage.

It’s in getting out of their way so they can actually get things done.

## Where this shows up

Every time I build something now, I’m thinking about this.

Not “how can I make this more?” but “how can I make this so simple it disappears?”.

The best tools aren’t the ones people spend time in. They’re the ones that solve the problem and get out of the way.

I’ve kept it simple in all of mine:
[Hndmark](https://hndmark.com/): Mark it up. Ship the feedback. Move on.  
[Winnny](https://winnny.com/): Log the win. See your progress. Carry on.  
[Soources](https://soources.com/): Get to the good stuff. Don’t wade through noise.

Fast. Simple. Done.

## The real opportunity

We’re drowning in tools and tech that demands our attention.

Dashboards that need checking. Interfaces that need learning. Features that need configuring.

The opportunity now isn’t building another one of those.

It’s making the messy bits actually work. Making the clunky thing simple. Removing the friction people have learned to just live with.

Because nobody’s asking for more features.

They’re asking for fewer headaches.

That’s where I’m focused.
